FAQ

Do I need to sign anything to qualify?
No. Eligibility is read from the blockchain at the snapshot. Never connect your wallet to a site that asks you to "verify" for a giveaway. We never DM first.
Can I use more than one wallet?
Each wallet is checked on its own and can win once per giveaway. Holdings across wallets are not combined.
When is the snapshot?
Ape Giveaway: when Inkbit Apes reaches 889 minted, or when the mint closes on Oct 29, whichever comes first. Garage: the date is posted on the Inkbit Apes OpenSea page before it happens.
How do I receive my reward?
Ape Giveaway winners receive the Ape in their wallet. Garage winners are added to an allowlist and claim their crate for free (network gas only).
Is this an investment?
No. Inkbit is an art collection. Prices can go down as well as up, and nothing here promises a return.
